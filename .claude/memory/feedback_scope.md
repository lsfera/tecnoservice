When adding new service sections, scope them tightly to exactly what the business offers — do not pad with extras.

**Why:** For the Spazzacamino (chimney sweep) section, the first draft included "Manutenzione caldaie a legna e pellet", "Ispezione con telecamera endoscopica", "Rilascio attestato di avvenuta manutenzione" etc. User corrected twice: "solo pulizia canna fumaria" and "senza rilascio attestato." The business only does flue cleaning, not full chimney services.

**How to apply:** Before listing service bullets, ask or infer from context exactly what the business does. Do not add plausible-sounding extras. When in doubt, write fewer bullets and let the user add more.
